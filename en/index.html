Pocket Timer TCG
Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

Pocket Timer TCG (the “app”) provides a match timer.

Information collected

The app uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google User Messaging Platform to display ads. Google’s SDKs may collect and transmit IP addresses (which may be used to estimate approximate location), identifiers associated with the device or app, information about ad displays and interactions, diagnostics, and performance information. Your ad privacy choices are used for consent management.

The app requests iOS tracking permission (ATT) for ad delivery and measurement. If you allow tracking, the app requests personalized ads using the advertising identifier. If you do not allow tracking, the app requests non-personalized ads. You can use the timer whether or not you grant permission. You can change this permission in your iPhone settings. The publisher first-party ID feature is disabled. Non-personalized ads may still involve communications and data processing needed for ad delivery, fraud prevention, and similar purposes. The app does not collect your contacts or precise location.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the match duration, remaining time, each player’s elapsed time, and display settings on your device. This information is used only to restore the timer state and is not transmitted externally. Deleting the app also deletes this information from your device.

Sharing with third parties

Information associated with ad delivery and consent management may be sent to Google and businesses involved in ad delivery. Timer settings, remaining time, and each player’s elapsed time are not transmitted.

Ad choices

A consent management screen is displayed according to your region and applicable requirements. When a way to change your choices is required, an ad privacy options control appears in the timer’s settings. You can use the timer even if ads fail to load or the device is offline.

Children’s privacy

The app is not offered as an app specifically for children. If it is offered for children in the future, the advertising and consent settings and this policy will be reviewed.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will announce the change on the public policy page or in the app’s update information.
