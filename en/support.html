Frequently asked questions

Can I use the app offline?
The timer works without an internet connection. Loading ads uses an internet connection.

Does the timer stop when I leave the app or lock the screen?
If the timer is running, the time spent in the background or with the screen locked is reflected when you return. To stop timing, pause the timer.

Will the screen lock automatically during a match?
In version 1.1.3 and later, the screen stays awake while the timer is running and the app is active in the foreground, including overtime and when you open settings. Normal auto-lock returns when you pause or reset the timer, or when the app becomes inactive or moves to the background. You can still lock your iPhone manually with the side button. Your iPhone’s system Auto-Lock setting is not changed.

What happens when I change the match duration?
The current timer and both players’ elapsed times are reset.
